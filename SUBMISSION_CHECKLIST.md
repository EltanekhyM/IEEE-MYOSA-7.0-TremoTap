# MYOSA 7.0 proposal submission checklist

- [ ] Confirm the organizers' current deadline and permitted format.
- [ ] Provide real team-member names, departments, academic years, contacts, and faculty mentor details in the private submission.
- [ ] Format the proposal in one column, A4, Calibri/Arial 11 pt, single spacing, at most four pages.
- [ ] Expression of Interest <= 200 words; Significance <= 100 words.
- [ ] Explain MYOSA sensors, actual and proposed software, hardware constraints, ethical limits, and a reproducible demonstration.
- [x] Public repository created: https://github.com/EltanekhyM/IEEE-MYOSA-7.0-TremoTap
- [ ] Confirm approval to publish any team contacts, images, firmware or human recordings.
- [ ] Treat early algorithms and datasets as unvalidated until appropriately tested.
